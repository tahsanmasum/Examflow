Built for real classrooms

📄

Upload any format

CSV, Excel, JSON, or manual entry. Paste questions or upload a file — ExamFlow parses and validates automatically.

📷

Photo question extraction

Take a photo of a printed question paper. ExamFlow reads the text and builds the exam for you — no typing needed.

🔗

One link, unlimited students

Generate a unique exam link. Share it anywhere — WhatsApp, email, classroom. Students open it in any browser, no app needed.

🔄

Smart shuffle system

Every student gets a unique version — question order and option positions are randomised per session. No two exams look the same.

⏱

Countdown timer & auto-submit

Set the exam duration. The timer counts down visibly. When time runs out, the exam submits automatically — no student action needed.

🔐

Anti-cheat built in

Tab switching, window blur, right-click, and keyboard shortcuts are all detected, logged, and shown to the teacher in real time.

📊

Live monitoring dashboard

Watch every student's progress, violations, and submission status in real time via Firebase. Updates the moment anything changes.

🌟

Instant results & grades

Students see their score, percentage, letter grade, and a full answer review the moment they submit — correct vs wrong vs skipped.

💾

CSV export & reports

Export all student results as a spreadsheet with one click — name, ID, score, grade, time taken, and violations included.
